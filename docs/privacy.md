# Privacy and data

## The rule

**This toolkit works on prose, tables and results you wrote. Never on raw data.**

The prompts refuse a pasted dataset. If your thesis rests on data you may not redistribute, that
restriction is doing real work for you, not getting in your way.

## Why it matters here

Charles University's principles on AI use prohibit entering personal, sensitive or otherwise
protected data into AI tools other than those the university has approved. Several IES theses use
licensed commercial data, bank or registry data, or data supplied under an agreement that says
plainly where it may go. Pasting any of that into a consumer chat product breaks the agreement and
possibly the university rule, and no feedback is worth that.

Summary statistics you computed and wrote up are your text. The underlying rows are not.

## No-paste mode

You can get most of the value without uploading your draft at all.

Fill in the Seminar Card by hand. Then paste only the card, plus your section headings, your
hypotheses, your table captions, and a description of your identification strategy in your own
words. That is enough for the contribution test, the design route, and most of the structural
checks. It contains nothing confidential.

The form sweep is the one thing this cannot do, because it needs the actual text. If your draft
cannot leave your machine, run the form sweep on chapters with the numbers redacted, or skip it
and ask a colleague.

## Which account to use

Check your provider's setting on training before you paste an unpublished draft, and re-check it
occasionally, because these settings and their names change.

If you would rather not use a personal account, the university provides access to Microsoft
Copilot under your CU account, which is the channel it names as approved for university work. It
is free to you. Using it means nobody is priced out of this toolkit, and it is the right choice if
your data is sensitive.

Even with training disabled, the provider receives the text. For anything embargoed, pre-patent or
under an agreement you have not read recently, use no-paste mode or do not use the toolkit.

## Nobody is required to use this

Using AI is optional in this course and is never assessed. `AI PRE-REVIEW: not run` on your
Seminar Card is a normal entry and costs you nothing. The card itself is fillable by hand, and the
thinking it asks for is the thinking the degree is meant to certify.

## What this repository contains

No student work. No referee reports. No names.

The design of this toolkit was calibrated against theses and referee reports published in the
Charles University repository, which are public documents. That calibration material stays out of
the repository, and the tool refuses to model or predict any named individual referee. See
`shared/evidence_discipline.md`.
